# Hi, I'm Sakhile Bhayi (SK) 👋

### Where Field Meets Digital

I'm a Mine Control Systems Engineer working at the intersection of **mining operations** and **software development** in Mpumalanga, South Africa. By day I keep systems running on an active coal operation; by night I build the tools I wish existed on site.

I grew up in Lydenburg (Mashishing) and I build software for the realities of South African mining — patchy connectivity, WhatsApp-driven workflows, and operational data that deserves better than a spreadsheet.

---

## 🔨 What I'm building

**⛏️ Mines** — a proprietary mining fleet intelligence platform ([mines.infodot.co.za](https://mines.infodot.co.za)). Fleet telemetry, operational dashboards, and a structured activity Feed designed to replace WhatsApp-based mine communications.

**🟣 Dot Suite** — a family of team-based productivity apps built on Laravel Jetstream Teams:
`Dot.Engage` · `Dot.Press` · `Dot.Forms` · `Dot.docs` · `Dot.Files` · `Dot.Sheet`

**📈 Trading tools** — MT5 Expert Advisors (MQL5) and Pine Script indicators and dashboards.

All shipped under the **Infodot** brand.
