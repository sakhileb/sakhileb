## About

My career spans both mining applications and modern software architecture. That dual fluency is the whole point: I don't build software *about* mining — I engineer the systems mining runs on, then architect the intelligence layer that learns from them.

The mines I worked for taught me what operational truth looks like. Software engineering taught me how to scale it. AI is how I multiply it.

## What I Engineer

**Fleet & Control Systems** — Real-time fleet monitoring, dispatch, telemetry, payload and cycle analysis, downtime intelligence. Built on deep OEM integration: Modular Mining Dispatch, Minecare 3, Provision, AVA.

**Executive Intelligence** — Production KPIs, shift and delay analysis, maintenance analytics, and performance scorecards designed for the people accountable for the numbers.

**AI Decision Systems** — Enterprise AI copilots, multi-agent systems, MCP architecture, RAG, and knowledge systems — practical AI that survives contact with real operations.

**Industrial Integration** — Telemetry streams, operational databases, and ERP systems unified into one intelligent platform.
